Join us

Whether you've played for years or haven't kicked a ball since school, we'd love to see you. We play in the Thames Valley Counties Women's Football League, Division 3 West, and home games are at Donnington Recreation Ground.

We train on Tuesday evenings in summer and Friday evenings in winter. Fill in our sign-up form and we'll be in touch before your first session.
